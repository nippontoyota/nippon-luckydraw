## 2. Program Period

2.1 The Program will run from **1st August 2026 to 31st August 2026** (both dates inclusive), hereinafter referred to as the "Program Period."

2.2 Only physical deliveries that occur within the Program Period at authorised Nippon Toyota outlets will be considered eligible entries.

## 3. Eligibility

3.1 The following categories of customers are eligible for the Program:

- Any customer whose vehicle physical delivery takes place between 1st August 2026 and 31st August 2026.
- Customers who made full payment before August 2026 but whose physical delivery falls within the Program Period.
- Customers who booked before August 2026 but whose physical delivery falls within the Program Period.
- Customers who make a booking during August 2026 and whose physical delivery also falls within the Program Period.

3.2 **All Toyota models** available at Nippon Toyota outlets are eligible for this scheme.

3.3 Employees of Nippon Toyota Pvt. Ltd., their immediate family members, authorised dealers, sales executives, and any agency associated with the Program are **not eligible** to participate.

3.4 Participation is restricted to individuals **18 years of age and above**.

3.5 A valid government-issued photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving Licence) and the original vehicle invoice/delivery document are required to claim any prize.

3.6 This scheme is **not applicable** to bulk purchases, institutional orders, or corporate fleet purchases.

## 4. How to Participate

4.1 A QR code will be placed at the Physical Delivery Bay at each Nippon Toyota outlet.

4.2 The QR code will also be shared with the sales team for customers opting for home physical delivery.

4.3 Eligible customers must scan the QR code and fill out the participation form. Sales representatives will assist customers in completing the form if required.

4.4 Upon successful form submission, the customer will receive an **acknowledgement message via WhatsApp**, containing a unique coupon number and the draw date details.

4.5 Customers will also receive a **link to the live draw event** (hosted on Instagram Live). This link will be sent twice — once immediately upon form submission, and again **one day before the draw date**.

4.6 Each eligible customer is entitled to **one participation entry** per vehicle delivery. Multiple entries for a single delivery will be disqualified.
