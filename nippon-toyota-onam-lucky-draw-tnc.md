## 2. Program Period

2.1 The Program will run from **1st August 2026 to 30th August 2026** (both dates inclusive).

2.2 Only physical deliveries that occur within the Program Period at authorized Nippon Toyota outlets will be considered eligible entries.

## 3. Eligibility

3.1 The following categories of customers are eligible for the Program:

- Any customer whose vehicle physical delivery takes place between 1st August 2026 and 30th August 2026.
- Customers who made full payment before August 2026 but whose physical delivery falls within the Program Period.
- Customers who booked before August 2026 but whose physical delivery falls within the Program Period.
- Customers who make a booking during August 2026 and whose physical delivery also falls within the Program Period.

3.2 **All Toyota models** available at Nippon Toyota outlets are eligible for this scheme.
